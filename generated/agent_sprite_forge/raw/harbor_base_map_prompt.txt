Base layer only. Do not include boats, stakes, numbers, dice, crates, barrels, signs, buoys, ropes, UI, text, labels, characters, or foreground props.

- clear open sailing corridor

- subtle harbor platforms and pier edges at the sides
- enough empty space for 10 future stake markers along the route
- keep the center route clean and readable
- avoid visual clutter where the boat will move
- make the map suitable for a 1280x720 viewport, with possible wider horizontal scrolling

Visual theme:
- Manila-inspired tropical harbor
- blue-green water
- warm sunlight
- weathered wooden dock edges
- concrete quay shapes
- subtle waves and painted water texture
- bright, friendly, stylized 2D game art

- base terrain and water only
